Sirimiri

Sirimiri (spanish) — light rain; a drizzle



Hand-in-hand We wait for the bus Her mascara dripping Our lips crying tears:

Lovers' closing bell— Unspoken word poetry— Kiss of forbidden love— —A farewell sirimiri

But that was long ago Now I wait for the bus alone

Still Sometimes I can feel the sirimiri on my lips But just for a second

Then Always after I feel a sirimiri falling from my eyes But not for a second
