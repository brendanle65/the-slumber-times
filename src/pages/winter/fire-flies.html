Fire Flies

Fire flies. The city winks. At dusk, its lights on one by one.

Shiny shoes wink. From girls in tight cocktail dresses Luring lads in neat leather jackets.

Chess hustlers wink. From the square where they play and drink and smoke and trash talk.

Fire flies. The city winks. At dawn, blocks go ablaze ten by ten.

Wedding rings wink. On severed fingers From severed hands.

Whole people wink. From fireball to fireball Babies burning on their backs.

Fireflies. Around the campfire.

A survivor winks. To his neices and nephews Fondling the moonlight.

He feigns gladness.
