The Beauty Store

Bins of balms—beauty psalms— lay near the checkout counter.

Come pray at the altar of alter. There are black dyes for redheads, and no blackheads or red skin with Kim K.'s magical solution, and if you pick up this tree oil, boiled at the perfect degrees with its "custom" hollywood formula, you can stop your hair from going tin and thin like a regular person.

No, No, No. We shall not age as Jesus commands: preserve your outside and reach inside your wallets and head to checkout counter number four. And you better have $300.56 for five items because to be poor, to be ugly, is one of the seven deadly sins.
