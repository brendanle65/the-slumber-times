Sunset

author’s note: this is a piece of fiction. it does not reflect the actual experience of the author.

The sky is purple.

I recall the morning red— When I listened to chirping cardinals— Fervently—in their robes and zucchettos.

I dream of the nightly blue— When I can feel the moonlight outside— Cancerfree—walking with my terrier.

The sky is purple— I feel a violent violet. God, why me?
