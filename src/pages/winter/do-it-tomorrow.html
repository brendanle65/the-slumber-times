Do It Tomorrow

why do i chain myself to the seat? if inspiration does not flow if the words do not go from aether to mind like a dipper why not go bowling or do some sowing? or just lay in bed and dream of tomorrow: when the pages are full of neat, cursive ink and the blink— ing nightlight does not taunt me but winks sincerely, pomp and cirumstance, for finding the muse. why do i chain myself to the seat?
