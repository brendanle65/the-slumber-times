God Did Not Invite Me To That Birthday Party

God did not invite me to that birthday party. My braces hurt. My acne bleeds. Why I talk funny? Why I walk funny?

Everyone laughs. In the locker room. In the hallways. In class.


God did not invite me to that birthday party.

Dad and I take the binocs and go watch birdys. Pretty ones. Bluejays. Cardinals.

I wish I had a hunting gun— Blast their wings and watch them thud.

Red grass.


God did not invite me to that birthday party.

I turn older today. My family made the cake. My friends are here—just two. Thank you Erik. Thank you Sasha.

It's time to blow out the candles. Son, make a wish.

I inhale. I exhale. Nothing more. No wish or prayer— I hate Him.


God— You're not invited to my birthday party.
