A Laminated Puzzle

Hanging on a wall Is a laminated puzzle.

It's beautiful— Galison Monet's Waterlillies. I pass it every day and admire.

But for the first time, I notice it's missing a piece In the lower left corner.

I can't unsee it. Thw whole is unwhole. That is how I am—

Like a laminated puzzle, All pretty and touched up But with a giant tiny hole.
