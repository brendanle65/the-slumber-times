Dimensions

author’s note: poem is inspired by Ron Padgett’s “Another One”

When you are a baby You experience only one dimension: Love Then later you learn There are three more: Height, width, and depth Like a shoebox But some folks never got the first So they only see three To them A dog is just an animal A poem is just words Then much later They learn about Einstein And they think they have found the fourth dimension: Time But no They are wrong Depth is the fourth; Time is the fifth; And love is the first

I kiss my wife goodbye I pet my dog I say good morning to the ladybugs I'm glad to live in five dimensions
