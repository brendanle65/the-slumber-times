Creations

lego flowers piece by piece we disassemble

rewind our valentine's gift back into technic stems and petal blocks

you take half i take half we are petty what's the point?

lego flowers piece by piece i reassemble

not that one a different set remixed more my style but just as pretty

i show you it and you share with me you've done the same

we exchange compliments and we renew correspondence sharing what we've built on our own

sometimes we even send pieces donations saying "remember this?"

i do you do how could we ever forget?
