Freckles

when God made you he consulted Michaelangelo: sculpted beauty with a chisel and dripped elixir from His vial, the pitty-patter of His work still on your cheeks.

like the edge spots of monarch butterfly wings, when in motion, (from your dipples smiling) the eye cannot help but track those spots; how they hoard attention from even the most scenic backdrops.

freckles, my dear. i don't want you any other way. i want you God's way.
