What If?

What if you wish upon a star and it twinkles?

What if you ask for more for extra sprinkles?

What if you take a break and taste some wine?

What if you go wild and cross the line?

What if you say "No" much more often?

What if you move away to bloody Boston?

What if you forget those stupid rumors?

What if you ignore all those boomers?

What if you ask the barista for more than coffee?

What if you stop masking and be a real softy?

What if— What if you finally tire of all the what ifs?

Of everything having to rhyme and follow a certain structure.

—

It may not work, but that's okay. You would have lived a life that's full, full of answers.

Not one spent staring out windows, watching the cars and taxis go by trotting over stone and puddles, hearing the echo:

What if? What if? What if?
