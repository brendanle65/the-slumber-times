Boudoir Voilà

In light acrylic paint, She poses in black lingerie: Areolas peek out the bra. This is her—sexy and raw.

In dark thick gouache, Her legs are embellished: Lite cellulite grows there. This is her—unsexy and raw.

Nude in her boudoir She admires the portrait. The artist did a good job. Voilà—This is me.

Voilà. Voilà. Voilà qui je suis.
