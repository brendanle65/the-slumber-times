Note To Self

this pain, this chasing condition, is not unbearable.

if you can the word, take it syllable by syllable, un-bear-a-ble the magical thing is the word becomes false. saying it makes it so, it means time has passed, four syllables later, and you are still breathing, still Alive.

this pain, this chasing condition, is not
