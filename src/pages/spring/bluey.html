Bluey

his name is Bluey. Bluey, the stuffed animal.

a baby blue penguin with a pudgy white body and cute yellow feet and beak.

he winks with both eyes those eyes shut tight. black slits saying "happy to be here" "happy to be with you."

his flipper are flying here: carefree; you can imagine him running and saying "Wee!"

he is soft but firm. one touch and you can't help but cuddle or talk.

with Bluey i'd talk about all kinds of stuff, stuff that only stuffed animals could ever understand: grown-up stuff love-life stuff life-ain't-going-so-well stuff, stuff upon stuff.

i know he ain't real but he feels feel and the moments with him are real.

a breadcrumb means nothing to you when you are stuffed but it's everything when you are hungry.

a breadcrumb means nothing to you when you are stuffed but it's everything when you are hungry.

and when you've been as hungry as I— every everybreadcrumb everybreadcrumbcounts.

so, for now, Bluey it is. Thank you Bluey I love you Bluey.
